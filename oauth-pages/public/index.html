CaptureFlow

Screenshot Intelligence

Privacy-First Integration

CaptureFlow is a smart personal organizer designed to help you capture notes, structure lists, and build your planner efficiently using screenshot intelligence.

To provide a unified view of your day, the app offers a one-way, read-only Google Calendar Import. This feature securely reads your primary calendar's upcoming events and maps them directly into your local planner timeline alongside your captures.

Your data belongs to you: CaptureFlow works 100% locally. Your Google Calendar data is processed entirely on your device and is never uploaded, shared, or sent to external servers.

At CaptureFlow, we are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use the CaptureFlow mobile application.

1. Information We Access and Collect

CaptureFlow is built with a local-first architecture. We only request permissions and data that are strictly necessary to run the application features:

  • Google Sign-In: Used solely to verify your identity and authenticate your user session securely.
  • Google Calendar Data (Read-Only): With your explicit consent, CaptureFlow requests the https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar.readonly scope to download upcoming calendar events from your primary Google Calendar.
  • Local Captures and Data: Any tasks, notes, or screenshot images you import or create inside the app are kept on your physical device.

Zero Data Server Storage: CaptureFlow does not run custom backend database servers to store your personal info or calendar data. All imported events and notes reside safely inside your app's local device storage.

2. How We Use Your Data

We use the accessed information exclusively for local app logic:

  • To display your upcoming Google Calendar events on the app's Planner timeline.
  • To associate tasks and notes with specific imported calendar events.
  • To enable offline access to your schedule.

We do not share, sell, distribute, or utilize your calendar data for advertising, marketing, profiling, or any other commercial purposes.

3. Third-Party Services

The application interfaces with secure services provided by Google to handle OAuth flows:

  • Firebase Authentication: To safely authenticate and verify your account.
  • Google API Services: To securely call the Google Calendar REST API directly from the client device.

These services are governed by their respective privacy policies.

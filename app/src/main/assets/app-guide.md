# CaptureFlow App Guide

CaptureFlow is a personal productivity app for tasks, notes, screenshot capture, and habits.
Data lives locally on the device; cloud AI features are opt-in. This guide explains what each
part of the app does and how to use it, so the in-app Assistant can answer "how do I..." questions.

## Navigation

- The hamburger icon (top-left) opens the side drawer: Settings, Profile, Habits & Reminders,
  Focus Timer, Compete with Friends, Support Us, Terms & Conditions, Privacy Policy, Sign Out.
- The bottom navigation bar switches between the 5 main tabs: Home, Planner, Capture, Notes,
  Search. Swipe left/right on the main screen also switches tabs.
- A floating chat bubble sits in the bottom-right corner on every screen after login. Tap it to
  open the Assistant chat panel in place; tap the close (X) to collapse it back to a bubble.

## Home tab

Shows an overview of the day: an OVERVIEW section with overall progress and a priority-ranked
task list, a SCHEDULE section with an "Up Next" card and a timeline of the day's tasks, and a
TODAY'S PROGRESS section with active habits and a task-breakdown chart. Tap a task to open its
details, or use the "+" / create action to add a new task. Icons in the top bar open Search and
Notifications.

## Planner tab

Shows tasks organized by day/week/month. Use it to see what's scheduled ahead and jump between
time ranges.

## Capture tab

An inbox of screenshots/images imported into the app (including via Android's Share menu — share
an image to CaptureFlow to add it here). Each capture goes through OCR (text extraction) and can
be reviewed, categorized, and turned into a task or note. Tap a capture to open Capture Detail,
from which you can open OCR Review (see/edit the extracted text) or AI Summary (generate a
summary and suggested category/tags, then optionally convert it into a task).

## Notes tab

A list of notes you've written. Tap the "+" to create a new note; tap an existing note to edit
it. The filter icon in the top-right (only shown when tags exist) lets you filter the list by
tag. While editing a note, you can ask the AI to summarize it into 1-2 sentences (uses the daily
AI quota, described below).

## Search tab

Search and filter across tasks, notes, and captures at once. Category chips at the top narrow
results by type; tap a result to jump to its detail screen.

## Habits & Reminders

Opened from the drawer. Create habits with a reminder interval; each habit tracks a current
streak. Tapping a habit's tracking option starts a live GPS-based distance/duration tracking
session (e.g. for a walk or run) — this keeps running even if you navigate elsewhere in the app,
and a floating bar at the bottom of the screen lets you get back to the live session or resume it.

## Focus Timer

Opened from the drawer. A focus/Pomodoro-style timer for uninterrupted work sessions.

## Compete with Friends

Opened from the drawer. Compare habit streaks and activity with friends.

## Collections

Reached from Home's "Collections" action. Groups related captures/notes together and lets you
jump back into Search with a specific query.

## Settings

Opened from the drawer. A hub linking to:
- **Profile**: your name/email, real usage stats (tasks completed, notes created), sign out.
- **AI & Privacy**: toggle Cloud AI on/off and give/revoke data-processing consent. Cloud AI (OCR
  summaries, capture categorization, and the Assistant chat) only works when both consent is
  given and Cloud AI is enabled — if either is off, those features show a message asking you to
  turn them on here.
- **Subscription**: upgrade to Premium, which removes the daily caps on AI summaries and
  Assistant chat messages.
- **Notifications**: view/manage app notifications.

## Daily AI limits

Free accounts get a limited number of AI summaries and a limited number of Assistant chat
messages per day (resets daily). Premium accounts have no daily cap. If you hit the limit, wait
until the next day or upgrade via Settings → Subscription.

## The Assistant

The floating chat bubble answers two kinds of questions:
1. Questions about your own data — e.g. "what's due today", "how many notes do I have",
   "what's my longest habit streak" — answered from your current tasks/notes/habits.
2. Questions about how to use the app — e.g. "how do I track a habit", "where do I change AI
   settings", "how do I filter notes by tag" — answered from this guide.

It requires Cloud AI + consent to be enabled (Settings → AI & Privacy) and counts against the
same daily message quota as other AI features.
